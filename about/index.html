Our Heritage

A Journey of Refinement

Founded three decades ago in the heart of the Swiss Alps, OPV Luxury began as a private concierge service for elite travelers seeking experiences that standard agencies could not provide.

What started as a boutique operation has evolved into a global ecosystem encompassing ultra-luxury stays, private aviation, and bespoke lifestyle management. Our growth has been organic, fueled by a singular promise: to handle the impossible with effortless grace.
